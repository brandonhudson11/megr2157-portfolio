# A9 – Pulley System Design

## Purpose
The goal of this assignment is to develop a pulley system that transfers power between two parallel shafts. The design must include two 3-inch pulleys positioned 4–6 inches apart and operate within a speed range of 1,000–2,000 rpm. The belt must maintain a stationary tension of 4–6 lbf and meet a minimum safety factor of 3. The project involves selecting an appropriate polyurethane belt, determining the required shaft dimensions, selecting journal bearings, and designing a supporting frame. Engineering calculations, sketches, and lessons learned will be used to explain and support the final design.

I decided to use steel for both pulleys, with a yield strength of 100,000 psi. The pulleys are designed to be 3 inches in diameter and mounted 5 inches apart from their centers. For the operating conditions, I selected a rotational speed of 1,000 rpm and a belt tension of 5 lbf. A safety factor of 3 was also chosen to provide an adequate margin against failure. These values will serve as the basis for determining the dimensions and specifications of the other components in the system.
