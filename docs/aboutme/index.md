#About Me
Hello, my name is Brandon Hudson, and I am currently junior studying Mechanical engineering with a concentration in motorsports at the University of North Carolina Charlotte!

I have known for several years now that I wanted to go down the path of engineering. Ever since middle school I have had the mind of an engineer and that is all I have wanted to become. The first thing that made me want to be an engineer was I wanted to work in nascar and I knew that would most likely require an engineering degree. Ever since I was 7, I have loved nascar and always dreamed of this profession. From middle school to high school math was always my favorite class and that is a strong interest to have for engineering. Although, I wanted to pursue engineering I always struggled to know, what is an engineer? Through many classes as now a Junior I have learned so much. In my first year of college, I was fascinated with the minds on engineers and how we think. I see myself as an engineer who is a problem solver and one who always thinks of ways to fix things rather than replace them. I personally enjoy the mechanics and machine process which is a well needed skill in mechanical engineering. 

I want to be an engineer who can solve daily problems by putting my skills to the test. From the moment I decided I wanted to become a mechanical engineer I have worked extremely hard to be able to be a problem solver. When I think of an engineer the words "problem solving" are the first things I think of. Other than wanting to pursue a career in nascar, another thing that fascinated me about engineering was the process of applying my math, physics, and building skills. I want to be an engineer who can build the best things and launch the best innovations. 

My dream is to become a nascar engineer and one day a crew chief. I want to be a racing engineer who can problem solve, collect data, and test new and improved things. The beauty of engineering is that you can fail, and your main job is to focus on where you failed and how you can now improve. I want to be able to solve real world problems and make anything I am tasked with better.
